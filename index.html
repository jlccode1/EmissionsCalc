Step 7: Understanding carbon credits

Carbon credits represent one tonne of CO2e reduced or removed from the atmosphere. When a company buys credits, it supports projects that deliver measurable climate benefits. These credits help balance unavoidable emissions while wider decarbonisation is underway.

Credits are created by verified projects such as renewable energy, nature-based solutions, and community initiatives. Each credit is tracked and certified to ensure credibility.

Companies use carbon credits to take responsibility for their footprint, meet stakeholder expectations, and finance climate action beyond their operations.

Renewable Energy

Wind, solar, and hydro projects that displace fossil fuels.

Reforestation & Conservation

Protects forests and restores ecosystems.

Community Development

Technology & Innovation

Carbon capture, biochar, and advanced solutions.
